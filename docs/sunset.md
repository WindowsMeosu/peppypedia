## Indefinite Hiatus

As of 04/06/2026, I am putting peppypedia on an indefinite hold. I'm not officially ending the project, as I may be interested in reviving it sometime, but at this moment, I have no interest in doing anything with peppypedia. On one hand, this is partly because there hasn't really been a motive for this project. It's largely dead. But on the other hand, I really cannot dedicate my free time to work on this project, especially now that I am much busier than I was a year ago.

## Liquidpedia

If you truly desire an encylopedia for osu!, I suggest you use Liquidpedia[^1]. It is genuinely a huge improvement over peppypedia and has a decent pool of information.

I do recommend that you contribute to Liquidpedia in any way you can; it is not only limited to osu!, but it is one of the best resources for esports.

That said, Liquidpedia and peppypedia operate on completely different terms. One is more competitive-centric; the other was made to document anything "osu!".

## Farewell

Whether I decide to continue peppypedia in the future, I appreciate everyone who has supported this project (even if that number is close to zero). 

I may continue to work on this project sometime again; I may not. I don't know, I don't have any interest at the moment, and nor do I have any motivation to consider doing so.

Farewell, for now.

Best regards, milotek.
